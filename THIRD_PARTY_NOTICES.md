# Third-party notices

First-party grants do not replace third-party licenses. See
[the installed production inventory](licenses/third-party/INVENTORY.json) for
exact versions, declared licenses, source repositories and copied upstream notice
paths. Preserve the upstream copyright/license notices when redistributing a
covered dependency. Sources and embedded notices in vendor archives remain intact.
An empty notice list means the installed package did not supply a notice file;
consult its declared upstream terms before public redistribution.

AI SDK provider-utils includes Stefan Terdell/Vercel's ISC-licensed
zod3-to-json-schema code. Undici includes Ethan Arrowood's MIT-licensed fetch
code. Those nested upstream notices are preserved separately. For provider-utils
and @workflow/serde, which declare Apache-2.0 (serde supplies an upstream pointer),
the full Apache license text is included alongside supplied notices. `json-schema`
offers AFL-2.1 OR BSD-3-Clause; use the BSD-3-Clause alternative and preserve its
supplied copyright/license file. Zod's supplied MIT notice is included.
